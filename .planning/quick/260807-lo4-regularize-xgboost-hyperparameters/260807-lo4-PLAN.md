# Quick Task 260807-lo4: Regularize XGBoost hyperparameters

## Motivation

A two-split benchmark (train 2022–23 → val 2024; train 2022–24 → val 2025) compared the
current calibrated XGBoost (n_estimators=500, max_depth=6) against LightGBM (default +
shallow), logistic regression, and a regularized shallow XGBoost (n_estimators=200,
max_depth=3, min_child_weight=5, subsample=0.8, colsample_bytree=0.8).

The shallow XGBoost beat the current config on both held-out years for both models:

| Task | Split | Model | Brier | Log loss | Winner NLL | Top-1 acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| prerace | val 2024 | current | 0.0433 | 0.3148 | 2.6706 | 0.433 |
| prerace | val 2024 | shallow | 0.0414 | 0.2428 | 2.6682 | 0.433 |
| prerace | val 2025 | current | 0.0310 | 0.1077 | 2.7174 | 0.550 |
| prerace | val 2025 | shallow | 0.0276 | 0.0979 | 2.6413 | 0.600 |
| inrace | val 2024 | current | 0.0503 | 0.2163 | 2.7694 | 0.365 |
| inrace | val 2024 | shallow | 0.0466 | 0.1848 | 2.7578 | 0.369 |
| inrace | val 2025 | current | 0.0306 | 0.1445 | 2.5883 | 0.771 |
| inrace | val 2025 | shallow | 0.0249 | 0.1095 | 2.5615 | 0.806 |

LightGBM-shallow ties shallow XGBoost in-race but loses pre-race; switching libraries is
not justified. Logistic regression is competitive pre-race only. Conclusion: keep XGBoost,
regularize it.
